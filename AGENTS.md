## Skills — Read Before Starting

Before starting any task:

1. Inspect `.agents/skills/` and identify skills relevant to the request.
2. Read the complete `SKILL.md` of every relevant skill before analyzing, planning, editing code, or running commands.
3. Follow the selected skill instructions throughout the task.
4. If no skill clearly matches, use `.agents/skills/find-skills/SKILL.md` to find the appropriate skill.
5. Do not claim completion until all verification steps required by the selected skills have passed.

These instructions are mandatory. Do not skip skill discovery or reading `SKILL.md`.
